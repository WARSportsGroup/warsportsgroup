About

Research and analysis on how hockey organizations actually work.

WAR Sports Group publishes long-form work on hockey operations: roster construction, front office structure, player development, culture, and the decisions that separate organizations that win from organizations that wait.

Most hockey writing is produced from the outside, looking in. This is written from inside the day-to-day operation of the sport, by someone who runs a rink, evaluates players, and has to make operational decisions that are graded every week.

Matthew Meyer

Founder, WAR Sports Group

I am the Director of Hockey Operations at a four-sheet facility in Metro Detroit, and I scout Michigan 16U and 18U AAA and high school hockey as a regional scout at the junior level. The work here comes out of both jobs: one teaches you what it takes to actually run a hockey operation on a schedule, with real people, real ice, and real money, and the other teaches you how thin the margin is between a player who makes it and a player who does not.

I write about organizations because that is the layer where most hockey conversations stop short. Rosters get debated endlessly. Structure, accountability, and the systems behind the roster rarely do. My long-term goal is hockey operations work at the team level, and this site is the visible portion of that work.
